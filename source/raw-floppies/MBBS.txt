Granted that every BBS is unique.  How then, do you mechanize the
process of logging in?
The solution is to create a database of login formats.
For example, the entry to log into TWM's MTHBoard is as follows:
mthbtwm 300 n 8 1 ATZ OK ATDT 884-8571 CONNECTED \r\r\r NAME? AEK
SSWORD? READER <wait 5> MMAND? READ <Start FROM> <End MMAND? <wait 10>>
<bye BYE> <wait 10> +++ <wait 5> CARRIER
The entry above describes the name of the system, the baThe entry above describes the name of the system, the baud rate, parity,
bits, Hayes modem codes, phone number, login name, and password.
It then proceeds to issue a READ command and begins downloading as soon
as it sees the word 'FROM'.  It continues downloading until it sees
the word 'MMAND?' with a 10 second pause.  It then issues a 'BYE' command
and waits 10 more seconds.  If logout attempt failed, it issues a Hayes
hangup code.
Standardization is not a problem.
